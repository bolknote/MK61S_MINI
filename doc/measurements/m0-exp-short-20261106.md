# RP2040 exp: short paths, 2026-11-06

HOST status: four previously losing/equal short paths now use fewer ARM
instructions than Qfplib. Subsequent [Pico measurements](m0-exp-short-pico-20261106.md) confirm all four timing wins.

The RP2040-only change replaces the positive input-scaling shift with its
exact two-word form (shift1..21), omits parity after the complete midpoint
band has already been rejected, and groups the decided mantissa increment
before the encoded exponent. The polynomial, error bounds, midpoint tests
and independent correctly rounded refinement remain unchanged. No tables
or static RAM are added.
